Collaboration Concert Ensuite and Trondheim

Collaboration Concert Ensuite and Trondheim

On the 19th of June Ensuite will close the academic year with an awesome collaboration with the Norwegian orchestra Symforch!

Symforch is the student symphony orchestra of Trondheim, Norway. In June, they travel to the Netherlands to explore our little country and bring some Norwegian culture to Eindhoven. After one week, they will give a collaborative concert with student symphony orchestra Ensuite of Eindhoven to bring their trip to a spectecular end. During the concert the entire audience will witness the bond between musicians of different nationalities.

The concert will take place at Augustinianum (Dirk Boutslaan 25) and starts at 15.00 hour. The halls open at 14.30 hour.

Repertoire:

Symforch

  • Piet Hein Rhapsody, Peter van Anrooy
  • Symfonisk Dans Op.64 Nr. 1, Edvard Grieg
  • Kjempeviseslatten, Harald Saeverud
  • Bojarenes Inntogsmarsch, Johan Halvorsen
  • Vorspiel till Hänsel & Gretel, Engelbert Humperdinck
  • Karelia Suite – 3.sats Alla Marcia, Jean Sibelius
  • Flaklypa Medley, Bent Fabricius Bjerre

Ensuite

  • Ricercare, Hendrik Andriessen
  • Kol Nidrei, Max Bruch
  • El Arca de Noe, Oscar Navarro

Concert:
19th of June 2022, 15:00
Augustinianum (Dirkboutslaan 25, Eindhoven)

Regular price: € 10.00

Student price: € 5.00
